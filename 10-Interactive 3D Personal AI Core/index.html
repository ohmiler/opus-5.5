Layer 1 — Identity

We build instruments for looking inside thinking machines.

Orrery is an independent research studio. We make the tools that let people see what a model is doing while it does it — not dashboards after the fact, but live, navigable views of computation.

Everything on this page runs on the same idea: a system you can steer, perturb and open up, and that answers back.
